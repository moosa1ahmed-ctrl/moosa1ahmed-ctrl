### About Me

I'm a final-year Computer Science student at Wilfrid Laurier University, concentrating in Artificial Intelligence and Software Engineering, expected to graduate December 2026. I like turning ideas into working systems and figuring out how things run under the hood, from gate-level logic circuits to backend services to reinforcement-learning agents.

Right now I'm building **ParkSense AI**, a B2B SaaS parking management platform tackling revenue loss from inefficient spot usage and theft, and helping drivers find a spot faster, especially in the underground, poor-connectivity environments most "smart" parking systems fail in.

- 🎓 Honours BSc Computer Science, Software Engineering & AI concentration, Wilfrid Laurier University
- 🅰️ Dean's Honour Roll (WLU, 2024/25) · Academic Distinction (WLIC, highest semester GPA)
- 🤖 Interested in robotics, AI/ML, embedded systems, and the automotive industry
- 💼 Looking for entry-level software and AI roles in the Waterloo / Toronto region
- 🌐 Full project breakdown, résumé, and transcripts: **[moosa1ahmed-ctrl.github.io/Portfolio](https://moosa1ahmed-ctrl.github.io/Portfolio/)**
